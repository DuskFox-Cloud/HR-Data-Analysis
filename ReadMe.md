# HR Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Human Resources data to uncover trends related to employee demographics, workforce distribution, and organizational structure. The goal of this analysis is to provide actionable insights that can help improve employee satisfaction, optimize talent management, and ensure strategic alignment with business objectives.

## 🎯 Objectives
* Analyze demographic and workforce data to understand employee distribution.
* Identify key trends in age, experience, and remote work adoption.
* Create an interactive dashboard for HR stakeholders to monitor key metrics.
* Provide data-backed recommendations for HR leadership to optimize workforce management.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), SQL
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI / Tableau
* **Reporting:** PowerPoint

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Workforce Scale & Distribution:** The organization has a total of 22,210 employees, with 19,000 currently active. The workforce is evenly divided by gender, with males and females each accounting for approximately 50.9%. 
* **Work Arrangement:** A significant majority (75.08%) of employees operate out of the headquarters, while 24.92% work remotely.
* **Age & Experience:** The active workforce has an average age of 38.44 years, indicating a mature and highly experienced talent pool. 
* **Departmental Focus:** The Engineering department represents the largest segment of the workforce with 5,854 employees, highlighting a strong technical focus within the company.
* **Diversity:** The data indicates a diverse work environment, representing a wide variety of racial and ethnic backgrounds.

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
Based on the data analysis, the following actions are recommended to leadership:
* **Expand Remote Work Options:** With only about a quarter of the workforce currently remote, exploring expanded remote work options could improve overall job satisfaction and help attract a broader talent pool.
* **Implement Mentorship Programs:** Leverage the deep experience of the mature workforce (average age 38.44) by creating mentorship initiatives to train and develop newer or younger staff.
* **Balance Departmental Resources:** Ensure that development opportunities and resources are distributed equitably across all departments, rather than being disproportionately concentrated in large sectors like Engineering.
* **Drive Diversity & Inclusion:** Continue to actively support and expand current diversity initiatives to foster varied perspectives and drive organizational innovation.
* **Continuous Monitoring:** Regularly review HR metrics and invest in advanced data visualization tools for dynamic exploration to adapt to changing workforce dynamics.

## 📂 Repository Structure
